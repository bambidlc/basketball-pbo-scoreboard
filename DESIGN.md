## Brand & Style

This design system serves a **live basketball scoring dashboard** — a professional-grade scorer's console used in real-time during basketball games. The visual identity is purpose-built for speed and clarity under arena conditions, where the operator needs to log actions instantly without visual ambiguity.

The aesthetic is **dark-mode control panel**: a near-black canvas that maximizes contrast for the bright team colors and stat indicators. Every element is designed for fast scanning — large monospace numerals for scores and clocks, color-coded team identity (red for away, blue for home), and semantically-colored action icons (lime for positive, red for negative, amber for fouls).

## Colors

The palette is strictly dark neutral with two dominant team accents and a semantic color layer for game events.

- **Surface Hierarchy:** The entire interface sits on `#0a0a0a` with containers stepping up through `#171717` and `#262626`. This three-tier surface system creates subtle depth without relying on shadows.
- **Team Identity:** Red (`#ef4444`) represents the away team and blue (`#3b82f6`/`#60a5fa`) represents the home team. These colors appear in headers, roster accents, event scores, foul dots, and possession indicators. They are the only saturated, large-area colors in the entire interface.
- **Semantic States:** Positive actions (made shots, assists) use lime green (`#84cc16`/`#a3e635`). Negative actions (missed shots, turnovers) use red (`#ef4444`/`#f87171`). Warnings and fouls use amber/yellow (`#fbbf24`/`#facc15`). Each stat action type has a unique color: offensive rebound (green), defensive rebound (sky), steal (violet), block (cyan), turnover (orange).
- **Text Hierarchy:** Primary text uses `#f5f5f5`, secondary uses `#d4d4d4`, muted labels use `#a3a3a3` or `#737373`. Micro-labels (10-12px) use black-weight (900) uppercase to remain legible at small sizes on dark backgrounds.
- **Court:** The basketball court surface is a distinct near-black with a slight blue tint (`#080a0d`), differentiated from the UI panels. Court lines render in light gray (`#d4d4d4`) at 2.7px with non-scaling stroke to maintain crispness at any zoom level.

## Typography

The system uses **Inter** as the primary UI font and **system monospace** for all numeric and data displays. The dual-font strategy separates human-readable content from tabular data.

- **Scores & Clocks:** Score numbers (60px) and the game clock (48px) use monospace black-weight (900) with `tabular-nums` for stable column alignment. These are the largest elements on screen and serve as the visual anchor of the dashboard.
- **Jersey Numbers:** Player jersey numbers (30px, monospace, black-weight) are the primary identifier in roster rows, placed leftmost for rapid scanning.
- **Labels:** All section headings, panel titles, and category labels use uppercase black-weight (900) at small sizes (10-12px). This creates a dense, technical aesthetic that maximizes information density.
- **Court Markers:** Shot location markers on the court use 9px monospace black text centered inside colored circles, displaying the point value (2 or 3).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is a **multi-zone grid dashboard** that adapts from a 2-column tablet layout to a 4-column scorer's workstation.

- **Grid Structure:** On large screens, the layout uses a 4-column grid: roster (250-280px), court (fluid), roster (250-280px), and scorer console (320-340px). The entire dashboard is capped at 1500px and centered.
- **1px Grid Dividers:** The grid uses `gap-px` with a `#262626` background to create hairline (1px) dividers between all major panels, giving a precise, engineered look.
- **Score Header:** The top header spans the first three columns, with the clock/match info centered between the two team blocks.
- **Court:** The central court panel stretches to fill available space with `minmax(0, 1fr)`, maintaining the SVG's aspect ratio via `preserveAspectRatio`.
- **Scorer Console:** The right column spans all three rows, creating a tall vertical panel for sequential game logging.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is minimal and achieved through tonal shifts rather than heavy shadows.

- **Surface Steps:** Panels alternate between `#0a0a0a` (outer) and `#171717` (interactive containers) to create a single step of elevation. Active/selected elements rise to `#262626`.
- **Outer Shadow:** The entire dashboard container uses a deep shadow (`shadow-2xl` at 40% black opacity) to separate it from the page background.
- **Court Overlays:** Floating info badges on the court use a semi-transparent `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.95)` background with a subtle shadow (`shadow-lg` at 30% black), creating readable overlays that don't obscure the court.
- **Active Press:** All buttons depress 1px on `:active` via `translateY(1px)`, providing tactile feedback for fast repeated inputs during live scoring.

## Shapes

The shape language is functional and restrained — rounded enough to feel modern but not so round as to feel playful.

- **Containers & Inputs:** Most elements use `rounded-md` (6px), providing a consistent corner radius across buttons, selects, cards, and input fields.
- **Outer Shell:** The dashboard container uses `rounded-lg` (8px) for a slightly softer outer edge.
- **Toggles:** Team and shot-mode toggles use `rounded-sm` (2px) for a compact, utilitarian feel.
- **Circles:** Foul indicator dots (6px), the shot clock display (64px), and active-player dots use `rounded-full` for circular elements.

## Components

### Score Header

The dominant visual element. Each team block is a large clickable area showing team name, label (AWAY/HOME) in team color, score in 60px monospace, foul count with dot indicators, and win-loss record. The center column shows the game clock (48px monospace), current period in amber, and match status. Selecting a team highlights the block with a darker background.

### Court Panel

An SVG basketball half-court with interactive shot-chart overlay. The court uses geometric precision rendering (`shape-rendering: geometricPrecision`) with a 760x440 viewBox. Court lines are drawn at 2.7px with non-scaling stroke. Shot markers are colored circles (8px for latest, 6px for history) with white borders and point-value labels. A crosshair cursor indicates tap-to-shoot interactivity. Three floating overlays show the selected shooter, shot mode (made/missed), and possession direction.

### Roster Panels

Left and right columns listing active players (top 5) and bench players. Each row shows jersey number (30px monospace), player name, points/fouls summary, and an active indicator dot in team color. Selected players show a checkmark and inset ring. A 4px left border in team color marks active (on-court) players. Rows are tall (66px active, 56px bench) to provide comfortable touch targets.

### Scorer Console

The rightmost column is the operator's control surface, spanning the full height. It contains: team toggle, player dropdown, player info card with points, shot mode toggle (made/missed), shooting foul checkbox, free throw counter (0-3), free throw buttons, stat action grid (assist, rebounds, steal, block, turnover, fouls, tech), substitution button, period selector, shot clock display, and a dev log panel showing sync status.

### Action Buttons

All action buttons follow a consistent pattern: dark container background (`#171717`), 1px border (`#262626`), medium rounded corners (6px), uppercase black-weight labels. Stat action buttons include a colored Lucide icon above the label. Hover states lighten the background. The selected state for toggles (team, shot mode, free throw count) uses a filled background with inverted or accent text.

### Event Feed

Recent game events display in a compact tabular grid: icon (color-coded), time (monospace), player name, action label, and running score in team color. Events scroll with a maximum of 6 visible rows.

### Dev Log Panel

A collapsed diagnostic section showing connection status (WiFi icon), sync message, and timestamped log entries. Log levels are color-coded: info (sky), success (lime), warning (amber), error (red). This panel provides transparency into the live data sync without disrupting the scoring workflow.
